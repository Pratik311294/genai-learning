SECTION 1: Company Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid leave per year.
Leave must be applied at least 3 days in advance.
Sick leave is separate and capped at 10 days per year.
Unused leave can be carried forward for a maximum of 5 days.

SECTION 2: Work From Home Policy
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.
WFH must be approved by the reporting manager.
Core hours of 10am to 4pm must be maintained during WFH days.
Internet allowance of Rs 500/month is provided for WFH employees.

SECTION 3: Performance Review Policy
Performance reviews happen twice a year — in June and December.
Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5.
A rating of 4 or above qualifies for a fast-track promotion.
All reviews must be completed within 2 weeks of the review period.
